How it works

  1. Launch through the page

    You sign one pump.fun create transaction in Phantom. The coin's creator is a burn wallet that LATTICE derives for that coin, so 100% of creator fees go to it. Platform cut: 0.

  2. A tick runs every 60 seconds

    It reads every coin's unclaimed creator fees and checks the timer. Interval depends on how much $LATTICE the launcher holds: 180 s by default.

  3. A burn fires when fees reach 0.005 SOL and the timer is due

    Below 0.005 SOL the coin shows "waiting for fees" and the timer keeps running. Nothing is skipped or lost; fees accumulate until the next due tick.

  4. One transaction does everything

    Sweep the burn wallet, collect the fees, buy the coin on its own curve, burn the tokens. One signature, one receipt, one Solscan link.

Docs

Burn wallet

Each coin gets its own burn wallet. The keypair is derived server-side from a master seed and the coin's mint; it is never shown and never leaves the server. On pump.fun the burn wallet is visible as the coin's creator, which is how it receives 100% of creator fees.

Timer

The tick job runs once a minute. A coin is due when now ≥ lastBurnAt + intervalSec. Interval is read from the launcher's $LATTICE balance at launch and re-checked on each tick: 180 s, 120 s at 1,000,000 $LATTICE, 60 s at 10,000,000.

Minimum burn

A burn needs at least 0.005 SOL of unclaimed creator fees. Below that the coin is marked waiting and nothing happens until the next tick.

The burn transaction

One versioned transaction: sweep the burn wallet, collect creator fees, buy on the coin's bonding curve (or via the migrated pool once it completes), then burn the bought tokens. The signature is the receipt. All receipts are listed above with Solscan links; nothing on this page is estimated.
